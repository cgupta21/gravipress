Frequently Asked Questions

How Gravipress v1.0 compresses files locally in your browser and what limits apply.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All parsing, rendering, image encoding, PDF optimization, and ZIP/GZIP packaging execute locally in your browser. Gravipress has no backend compression server and no user accounts. See the Privacy Policy for static hosting details.

What formats and file limits are enforced?

Gravipress v1.0 supports PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF (browser dependent), ZIP, and GZIP. Audio (MP3) and video (MP4) compression are not supported in v1.0. Enforced limits:

Mode / Resource Enforced Limit
PDF 100 MB max size, 200 pages max, 8192 px max side, 24 MP max rendered page
Images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF) 50 MB max size, 8192 px max side, 32 MP max decoded pixels
Batch Queue 20 files max, 100 MB combined input
ZIP Archive / Folders 250 files max, 100 MB combined input, 50 MB max single file, 12 folder levels
GZIP Archive Single file up to 50 MB

Why can't some files be compressed further?

Files that are already compressed (such as pre-optimized JPEGs, PNGs, ZIPs, or encrypted/random binary data) often have little redundant data left. When Gravipress cannot produce a smaller valid output, it retains the original file instead of enlarging it.

How do PDF presets and rasterization work?

Light mode applies structure-only optimization (object streams) and preserves selectable text, links, and forms. Balanced (~144 DPI) and Maximum (~96 DPI) try structural optimization first and, for image-heavy PDFs, may rasterize pages into compressed JPEGs. Rasterized pages cannot retain selectable text, forms, hyperlinks, or accessibility tags.

What happens with encrypted or digitally signed PDFs?

Password-protected or encrypted PDFs are rejected because they cannot be safely rewritten without decryption. Modifying or rasterizing a digitally signed PDF may invalidate its cryptographic signature—always keep your original signed copy.

Does image compression remove EXIF metadata?

Yes. Re-encoding an image through the browser Canvas API strips EXIF, IPTC, and XMP metadata (such as camera model and GPS coordinates) and normalizes color output to sRGB.

Why does AVIF work in some browsers and not others?

AVIF encoding relies on your browser's native canvas.toBlob('image/avif') implementation. Browsers without built-in AVIF encoding support cannot output compressed AVIF files.

Does Gravipress work offline?

Once the page is loaded in your browser tab, compression continues to work without an internet connection. However, Gravipress v1.0 does not currently install a Service Worker for offline cold-starts after closing the tab.
