Gravipress is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis, without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement. Compression results depend on your input files, browser capabilities, and device memory.

2. Keep Backups of Original Files

Always retain backups of your original files before compressing or replacing them. You are solely responsible for verifying that compressed outputs meet your visual, structural, and archival requirements.

3. Quality, Metadata, and Digital Signatures

  • Lossy compression & rasterization: Balanced and Maximum presets for images and image-heavy PDFs trade visual fidelity for smaller file sizes. Rasterizing PDF pages removes selectable text, forms, hyperlinks, and accessibility tags.
  • Metadata stripping: Image re-encoding removes EXIF, IPTC, and XMP metadata and normalizes output to sRGB.
  • Digital signatures: Processing a digitally signed PDF may invalidate its signature.
