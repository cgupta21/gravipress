How much pressure?

Tries structural optimization first, then ~144 DPI (JPEG 0.75) with bounded adaptive tuning if needed.

Re-encoding removes EXIF/IPTC/XMP metadata and normalizes to sRGB.

WebM output via native WebCodecs. Opus audio is preserved when supported. Max 100 MB / 300s.

Opus compresses audio lossily. WAV converts to uncompressed 16-bit PCM.
